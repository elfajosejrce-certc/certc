Quick answers

Before you enroll.

Need a more specific answer? Message our Facebook page and the CERTC team will assist you.

Who can enroll at CERTC?+

Engineering graduates preparing for licensure exams, students strengthening their foundations, and professionals pursuing technical or safety training may enroll in applicable programs.

What learning modes are available?+

Availability depends on the program. CERTC offers face-to-face, live online, and self-paced options across selected review programs.

How do I secure my slot?+

Open the pre-enrollment form, complete the required details, follow the payment instructions, and send your proof of payment to the CERTC Facebook page for confirmation.

Where is CERTC located?+

CERTC is at 3F, 7J Corporate Center, Jalandoni corner Ledesma Street, Barangay Gloria, Iloilo City.
